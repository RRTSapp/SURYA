# ☀️ SURYA – Solar Utility Resource & Yield Architect

### An Integrated Solar Park Master Planning and Engineering Platform

**SURYA (Solar Utility Resource & Yield Architect)** is a comprehensive engineering platform designed to simplify, automate and standardise the planning and design of utility-scale solar power parks.

Developed with a focus on practical solar EPC engineering workflows, SURYA brings multiple stages of solar park development into a unified application — from terrain assessment and infrastructure planning to site subdivision, detailed solar array layout and consolidated engineering outputs.

### 🚀 Key Capabilities

- **Terrain Analysis:** Analyse site boundaries, terrain characteristics, elevation and developable areas.
- **Park Infrastructure Planning:** Plan internal roads, access roads, drainage networks, electrical infrastructure and other common facilities.
- **Site Subdivision:** Divide large solar parks into manageable engineering and development blocks.
- **Solar Site Layout:** Generate optimised module/table arrangements, DC capacity configurations, inverter placements and electrical cable routing.
- **Drainage Engineering:** Plan drainage networks with sub-channels and main collector channels, taking terrain and site conditions into account.
- **Master Consolidation:** Consolidate individual site layouts, infrastructure and engineering metrics into an integrated park-level output.
- **Project Save & Restore:** Save and restore complete project information using portable JSON project files.

### 🏗️ Engineering Approach

SURYA is being developed as a modular, engineering-oriented platform. Its objective is to reduce repetitive design work, improve consistency across projects and provide a structured workflow for solar park planning.

The platform is intended to support different project capacities, site geometries and terrain conditions rather than being restricted to a particular solar project or location.

### 🛠️ Technology Stack

### 🎯 Vision

To build an integrated digital engineering environment for utility-scale solar parks — bringing terrain intelligence, infrastructure planning, solar array design and engineering coordination together in one platform.
